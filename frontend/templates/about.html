How The Model™ V4 works

Every line on this site is a count.

Each week I play every matchup in the league 50,000 times. If a team wins 32,000 of them, it's a 64% favorite and its moneyline is −178. No bookie, no house edge, just counting.

How a week gets priced

  1. Collect the projections

    Eight sources, pulled midweek and again after Thursday's game. Each one is checked against Sleeper before it counts: the wrong week, scrambled positions or numbers way off everyone else's, and that source sits the week out. I won't name them all because my wife is not yet bar-certified.

  2. Correct for each source's lean

    Every source leans somewhere. One projects quarterbacks too high, another lowballs running backs. Graded against last season's real scores, each source is corrected for its lean at every position and weighted by how close it usually lands.

  3. Give every player a range

    A projection is a guess, so each player gets a distribution instead of a number. It leans right, because boom weeks run bigger than bust weeks, and it carries a real chance of a dud: the receiver hurt on the first drive, the backup who never sees the field. Teammates move together, so when a quarterback goes off, his receivers usually do too.

  4. Set the lineups

    Each team starts its best healthy lineup. Byes, injuries and IR are skipped, and an empty slot is filled from waivers in FAAB order, the way a real owner would before kickoff.

  5. Play the week 50,000 times

    Every starter's score is drawn, each team's scores are added up, and every market is judged in every one of the 50,000 versions of the week. The odds are how often each thing happened.

What you can bet

Moneyline
Who wins the matchup.
Spreads
Any line from −50 to +50, each priced at that line.
Over/Under
A team's total, set at its median so both sides are even money.
Highest & Lowest Scorer
The week's top and bottom team.
Parlays
Two to four picks, priced on the sims where every one of them hits, so picks that go together aren't priced as if they don't.
Futures
First place, making the playoffs, last place and the title, from 20,000 simulated rest-of-seasons.

House rules

  • When. New odds go up midweek and again after Thursday's game. Betting pauses at each kickoff until the next update and locks for the week at Sunday's first kickoff.
  • Changing your mind. Until the odds update, cancel any bet for a full refund. After they update, cash out for 95% of what it's worth at the new odds.
  • Settling. Bets settle from Sleeper's final scores after Monday night. Landing exactly on a line is a push, and the stake comes back.
  • Stakes. The money is fake and everyone starts with $1,000. The prizes are real: $20 for the week's top net profit, $100 for the season's.

Is it any good?

Before switching to V4 I tested it on 2025's weeks 10–16, predicting each week from the others. A player's 80% range should catch 80% of real scores.

Last season's model 66%
The Model™ V4 77%
80% target

Team totals land inside their 10th–90th percentile range 79% of the time, and the average miss on a team's score is about 20 points. Fantasy football is noisy, which is the whole reason there's something to bet on. The moneylines came out a little sharper than last season's, but not yet by enough to brag about.
