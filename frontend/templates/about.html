How the odds are made

Every price on TNCasino comes from simulating the week 50,000 times. A team that wins 32,000 of those simulations is a 64% favorite, priced at −178. No house edge is applied.

The Model™ V4

  1. Data science modeling

    Projections come from eight sources, each checked against Sleeper before it's used. Every source is corrected for its historical bias at each position and weighted by its past accuracy. I won't name them all because my wife is not yet bar-certified.

  2. Player distributions

    Each player gets a right-skewed distribution around their projection, with a fitted probability of a near-zero game. Teammates' scores are correlated.

  3. Simulation

    Every starting lineup is simulated 50,000 times. A market's odds are the share of simulations in which it wins.

Rules

  • Odds update midweek and after Thursday's game. Betting pauses at each kickoff and locks at Sunday's first kickoff.
  • Bets can be cancelled for a full refund until the odds update.
  • Bets settle on Sleeper's final scores. A result exactly on the line is a push.
  • Everyone starts with $1,000 in fake money. Prizes are real: $20 for the week's top profit, $100 for the season's.
